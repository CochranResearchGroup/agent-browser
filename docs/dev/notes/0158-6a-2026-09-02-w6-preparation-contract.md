partially bound provider manifests fail required-provider readiness.

The campaign controller now binds 18 required artifact classes, two exact
environment seals, the C01 calibration and frozen budgets, and an aggregate
fixture manifest. Its immutable freeze receipt records the actual wall and
monotonic time plus zero started cases and attempts. E2 ingress is required for

alone. It rejects bad hashes or byte counts, candidate or environment drift,
missing receipt kinds, invalid calibration chronology, local or duplicate
clients, any missing DNS/TLS/redirect/cookie/WebSocket/iframe/form-action/
reconnect evidence, a non-clean W4 report, and a non-pristine controller. It
does not repair or start a case.
