# Teaching and continuation capability boundary

Product lane: PL-RECIPES
Disposition: active-input
Owning work item: [Establish the teaching and continuation capability boundary](https://github.com/CochranResearchGroup/agent-browser/issues/444)
Related lanes: PL-PLATFORM, PL-AUTH, PL-CHALLENGE
Research date: October 25, 2026

## Question, scope and stopping rule

Can existing Agent Browser, Remote View and Speech Receipts compose narrated demonstration, reviewed SiteGuide and verified repeat execution, while retaining interruption-safe continuation? This bounded read-only investigation used owning source, specifications, current GitHub issues and attributable acceptance notes. It stops at a capability/evidence classification and explicit gaps; it does not select human product tradeoffs or implement the composition.

No private media, credentials or runtime configuration was read. No capture, inference, browser, build or service action was run. Reported installed outcomes below are recorded owner evidence, not independently rerun experiments.

## Finding

The essential primitives exist. A new recorder, transcription service, credential authority or procedure registry is unnecessary. The missing product work is the cross-package composition: select and authorize the demonstration artifacts, preserve a common evidence timeline, transform reviewed observations/narration into candidate SiteGuides and routines, and verify repeat execution. Existing continuation mechanisms preserve task/effect custody; they do not themselves supply a general task briefing or automatically recover every transient page state.

## Missing joins and constraints for the human decisions

1. No Remote View recording-to-Speech ingestion adapter or teaching-session orchestrator was established in the inspected interfaces. Speech's current source adapter resolves its own Saved/temporary-upload custody; it does not accept an arbitrary Remote View recording ID or grant. A narrow adapter can assemble authorized bounded slices, verify finalized hashes and submit one intentional owned media operation. Whether to retain a Speech copy or use an authorized source adapter remains a product/contract decision.
2. Preserve external recording ID, exact source artifact/hash, selected track, revision, conversion offset/padding and time mapping. Speech currently extracts the first audio stream; explicitly exporting the narration source avoids assuming the first mixed track is narration. Its default five-minute decoded/range limit is shorter than Remote View's recording bound. Segment longer teaching sessions explicitly and preserve offsets; do not silently truncate them. [Media conversion](https://github.com/CochranResearchGroup/speech-receipts/blob/0bc273db691894402353ed7c5219949755b3c65c/docs/media-conversion.md).
3. Input events indicate sent_to_transport, not successful application actions, and exclude clipboard content. Narration may be wrong or mistranscribed. Candidate generation therefore needs semantic observations and outcome evidence, reviewed transcript corrections and uncertainty/gap annotations. Existing source tracks do not establish who a speaker is.
4. No end-to-end narrated demonstration-to-candidate-to-reviewed-repeat workflow was found in the bounded current contracts. Existing SiteGuide preparation and candidate validation are a foundation, not proof of automatic derivation. Retain source provenance/version history and make review/correction a real step before repeat execution.
5. Existing login checkpoint and control-return mechanisms should carry forward rather than be replaced by a generic replay engine. A task briefing would need a compact projection of goal, current exact target, last verified outcome, pending assistance, artifacts and unresolved effects. A saved tab or cookie does not preserve unsaved form state or authorize replay of a submission.
6. Secret-sensitive recording periods need an explicit contract with Auth and existing capture controls. An operator-owned evaluation privacy exception is not a claim that independent recordings/viewers are suppressed. Private values must not become guide content, public research artifacts or narration examples.

These are evidence gaps and decision inputs, not adopted implementation requirements. The next human decision can set measurable acceptance for one recorded synthetic demonstration, reviewed guide and successful fresh repeat, plus controlled interruption and stale-site cases, while preserving independent actual-microphone and provider acceptance.
